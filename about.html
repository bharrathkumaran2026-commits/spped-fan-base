The rise to fame

iShowSpeed is the online name of Darren Jason Watkins Jr. He created his YouTube channel in 2016 and, around the end of 2017, began live streaming games such as NBA 2K and Fortnite to a tiny audience.

By his own account, he gave up almost everything else to stream every day. His channel passed 1 million subscribers on June 30, 2021, and 10 million in July 2022. Loud reactions, unscripted chaos and viral clips did the rest.

His shout "SEWY!", inspired by his idol Cristiano Ronaldo, became his signature. Fans now chant it back to him in stadiums and city squares around the world.

YouTube milestones

  1. 2016 – 2017: The beginning

    Created his channel in 2016 and started livestreaming games to a very small audience.

  2. June 2021: 1 million subscribers

    Viral stream clips pushed his channel past its first million.

  3. August 2021: First single

    Released the song "Dooty Booty" on his channel. The video has gone on to receive well over 160 million views.

  4. July 2022: 10 million subscribers

    Grew tenfold in about a year and later received the Diamond Play Button from YouTube's CEO.

  5. 2023: The world tour begins

    Started streaming live from other countries, beginning in Europe and then Southeast Asia.

  6. 2024 – 2025: Bigger stages

    Appeared at major sporting events including WWE WrestleMania, and took his streams to Asia, including China in March 2025.

  7. January 2026: 50 million subscribers

    Passed 50 million live on stream in Lagos, Nigeria, during the Speed Does Africa tour, on his 21st birthday.

  8. July 2026: 60 million subscribers

    Reached 60 million after his FIFA World Cup 2026 tour streams brought in millions of new fans.
