Quick answers

What does "SEWY" mean?

It is Speed's famous celebration shout, inspired by his idol Cristiano Ronaldo's celebration. Fans shout it back at him in cities around the world.

What does he stream?

Mostly live YouTube streams: games like NBA 2K and Fortnite, reactions, challenges and, since 2023, real-life world tours.
